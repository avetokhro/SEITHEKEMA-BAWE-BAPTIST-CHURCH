About Our Church

Growing together in Christ

Seithekema Bawe Baptist Church is located at Seithekema Old Village, Chumukedima, Nagaland. We gather to worship God, learn from the Holy Bible, encourage one another, and share the good news of Jesus Christ.

Whether you are a regular member, a returning visitor, or joining us for the first time, you are welcome to worship and fellowship with us.

“For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.”
MATTHEW 18:20 (KJV)
